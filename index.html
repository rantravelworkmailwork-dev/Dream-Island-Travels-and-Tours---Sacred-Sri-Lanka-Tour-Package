Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport (~12:00 PM noon) and receive a warm welcome from your private chauffeur guide. Drive directly north to the ancient sacred city of Anuradhapura. Check in to your lakeside resort and relax for the evening.

🤝 Airport Meet & Greet 🚗 Scenic Drive to Anuradhapura
Overnight: Anuradhapura — Miridiya Lake Resort
Meals: Dinner included

Morning Conduct pilgrimage vows and offer special Puja prayers at the sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi tree and surrounding shrines in Anuradhapura.

Afternoon Proceed to Habarana for an exciting 4x4 Habarana Elephant Safari followed by an authentic Habarana Village Tour with an authentic Sri Lankan Traditional Lunch.

🛕 Sacred Bodhi Tree Puja 🐘 Habarana Elephant Safari 🌾 Habarana Village Tour 🍛 Sri Lankan Traditional Lunch
Overnight: Sigiriya — Cassandra Culture Resort
Meals: Breakfast, Traditional Lunch & Dinner included

Morning Climb the majestic 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress. Afterwards, transfer towards Kandy (Optional stops available en route at Dambulla Cave Temple or a Matale Spice Garden).

Evening Pay homage at the sacred Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic in Kandy and watch a traditional Cultural Dance Show.

🥾 Sigiriya Rock Fortress 🛕 Temple of Tooth Relic 💃 Cultural Dance Show
Overnight: Kandy — Hotel Senani Kandy
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner included

Visit a working Tea Plantation & Tea Factory to learn about traditional Ceylon tea manufacturing (Optional visit to Gem Museum). Head to the station to board the world-famous Highland Scenic Train to Ella winding through misty tea fields.

☕ Tea Plantation & Factory 🚂 Scenic Train Ride to Ella
Overnight: Ella — Ella Flower Garden Resort (or Hillcrest Ella)
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner included

Spend a full day exploring Ella's natural wonders. Visit the world-famous Nine Arch Bridge to watch trains cross the mountain gorge, and hike up Little Adam’s Peak for panoramic views across Ella Gap.

🌉 Nine Arch Bridge Visit ⛰️ Little Adam’s Peak Hike
Overnight: Ella — Ella Flower Garden Resort (or Hillcrest Ella)
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner included

Enjoy breakfast in Ella and check out. Travel down through the hills toward Lenagala. Arrive at the Lenagala Meditation Center for your overnight spiritual stay and peaceful reflection.

🚗 Scenic Transfer to Lenagala 🧘 Meditation Center Stay
Overnight: Lenagala — Staying overnight at the Meditation Center (No hotel booking required)
Meals: Center Dining / Local arrangement

Depart Lenagala for Colombo. Enjoy a guided Colombo City Tour taking in historic colonial architecture, Independence Square, and Pettah bazaar before transferring to Negombo for a coastal evening.

🏙️ Colombo City Tour 🏖️ Negombo Coastal Stay
Overnight: Negombo — Earl's Regent Negombo
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner included

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast by the beach at Earl's Regent Negombo. Check out and take a quick private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure flight (~12:00 PM noon).

Departure Transfer: Private transfer to airport.
Meals: Breakfast

Important Notice Regarding Train Travel

Train services in Sri Lanka are subject to seat availability and may be affected by maintenance, scheduling changes, or unforeseen circumstances beyond our control. In the event of train cancellation or unavailability, comfortable alternative road transport will be provided by your private vehicle.

If train travel cannot be arranged under any circumstances, we will refund the cost of your train ticket.

Anuradhapura Entrance Ticket Notice

The standard entrance ticket for the Anuradhapura sacred sites is $30 USD per person. However, since early morning visits for Puja ceremonies do not require a ticket, this fee is not included in the tour package cost.

Pilgrimage Puja Offerings Assistance

These items can be bought straight out at a shop in Sri Lanka. The chauffeur will assist in the process, but no cost is held by the travel agency. The flowers can also be bought near the temple.
